What

I study how robots keep learning about the physical world through interaction after deployment.

Why

I’ve spent my life hardcoding adaptability, into robots and into myself through field hockey. What excites me is machines that can learn it the way I had to.

  1. Mostly self-taught, by asking engineers, professors, and other competitors.

  2. Every run, the robot meets a field it hasn’t seen. We won the RoboCupJunior USA Rescue Line title in 2023, 2024, and 2025. Team FATEN’s site →

  3. Robots outside competition: Alstom in Singapore, Blue Innovation in Japan, and the Okada Lab in Tokyo.

  4. Transferred from Bard College at Simon’s Rock, where I studied mathematics. B.S. expected May 2027.

  5. Moving up to the major league, building toward 2027.

  6. The first step from hand-written fixes to robots that learn what went wrong. With Irmak Guzey in Prof. Lerrel Pinto’s group at NYU, I’m building a test of whether a simulated robot lifts more reliably when it refines its estimate of an object’s mass by interacting with it.
